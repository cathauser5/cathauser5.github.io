About Me

I'm a Ph.D. student at Boston University and a self-appointed ecological software engineer. After finishing my undergraduate degree in Biomedical Engineering in 2020, I worked for four years in the tech industry as a customer support analyst, implementation engineer, and as a backend software engineer. In September 2024, I began my Ph.D. at Boston University in the Biology Department. I am now working on my dissertation which involves modeling coral reef ecosystems, field experiments, coral restoration work, and lots of scientific diving. My academic and professional goals are to merge the worlds of coding, diving, and coral research together to study and (hopefully) develop solutions to the problems plaguing coral reefs.

I'm in the Kaufman Lab at Boston University and I'm co-advised by Dr. Les Kaufman and Dr. Ethan Deyle.
