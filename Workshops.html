Internships & Workshops

I completed an internship from August 2026 to August 2027 in collaboration with Fragments of Hope, USF and BU. The goal of this work was to explore the potential use of hybrid reefs in Caye Caulker, Belize. We wanted to start this work by reaching out to the Caye Caulker community directly to discuss their thoughts on hybrid reefs and if they felt Caye Caulker would benefit from using them.

In this internship:

  • I organized and led a workshop in Caye Caulker, Belize on February 24-25, 2026, in collaboration with Ali Cansino (Fragments of Hope) and Alex Webb (University of South Florida) and the NSF Strong Coasts project. This workshop engaged the community on the potential use of hybrid reefs in the area. This video shows a recap of the workshop.
  • I analyzed feedback results to create an accessible Story Map detailing the workshop and site visits.
  • I worked with the team to created a comprehensive public facing project report. This report includes a workshop overview, the data analysis, and preliminary monitoring and safety plans.
  • These results were all shared results with the Belize Fisheries Department.
  • At the NSF Strong Coasts annual meeting, I presented a summary of the work to the Belize Fisheries Department in June 2026.
